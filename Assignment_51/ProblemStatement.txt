demonstrates the power of ensembel learning using a Voting Classifier with models like Logistic Regression,
Decision Tree.

Dataset Information:
Dataset Name: Fake News Dataset

        Soft Voting(average predicted probabilities)

    Display confusion matrices
    Soft vs hard Voting
